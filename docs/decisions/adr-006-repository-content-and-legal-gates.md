# ADR-006: Repository, Content and Legal Gates

**Status:** Proposed / pending Cyrus exact-SHA approval

## Context
The public repository mixes Apache-licensed code, upstream materials and reserved business content. Public claims, founder facts and legal text require controlled provenance.

## Options
Treat everything as Apache-2.0; keep content outside repository; maintain explicit path-level scope, rights and review gates.

## Proposed decision
Maintain Apache-2.0 for Jamula reusable code, preserve upstream licenses/notices, and reserve Jamula marks, brand assets, copy, case studies and customer materials through explicit scope/inventory. Use the editorial workflow, asset rights ledger, claims register and founder source register. Internal themed identities never appear in public branding. Legal pages are requirements/drafts only until counsel approval.

## Consequences
More recordkeeping and professional review; lower infringement, endorsement, confidentiality and misleading-claim risk.

## Reversibility
Remove or replace unapproved content/assets; preserve prior granted rights; version approvals and propagate corrections/takedowns.

## Portability / exit
Original source assets, rights metadata, content IDs, canonical URLs and channel derivatives remain Jamula-controlled.

## Data export
Export content, approvals, rights/releases, claims, corrections and publication logs subject to privacy, retention and legal hold.

## Cost
Include rights acquisition, accessibility derivatives, moderation, professional IP/privacy/advertising review and platform migration.

## Confidence
High in process requirement; professional conclusions and all public wording remain pending.

## Dissent / tradeoffs
Public repository transparency aids trust/reuse but can unintentionally license or expose content. External-only content reduces exposure but fragments provenance.

## Reconsideration trigger
Counsel advice, license/rights ambiguity, new asset/channel/customer permission, claim correction, repository license change or trademark use.
