# Jamula Operating Principles

**Status:** Provisional - not authoritative until Cyrus approves this exact version.

This is original Jamula language inspired by the intent of Amazon's official Leadership Principles and Microsoft's official mission, values, growth mindset, inclusion, trust, accessibility, responsible AI, and sustainability commitments.

1. Start with customer outcomes.
2. Own the whole system.
3. Learn, invent, and simplify.
4. Decide with evidence and diverse perspectives.
5. Act with urgency and judgment.
6. Raise quality and deliver.
7. Earn trust through respect, integrity, and accountability.
8. Challenge respectfully, then commit together.
9. Think boldly and stay resourceful.
10. Grow people, partners, and community.
11. Build responsibly for people and planet.
12. Scale responsibility with capability.

## Decision behavior

Every major issue or ADR records customer impact, owner, evidence, dissent/tradeoffs, privacy/security/accessibility impact, environmental/social impact, cost, and measurable outcome.

Frugality never overrides safety, rights, accessibility, fair treatment, commercial permission, or legal obligations.
