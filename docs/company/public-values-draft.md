# Public Values Draft

**Status:** Provisional. Not approved for publication.

Jamula aims to:

- begin with customer outcomes and earn trust through honest, accountable delivery;
- build secure, private, accessible, and inclusive technology;
- use resources deliberately and prefer efficient, maintainable solutions;
- evaluate AI and data use responsibly, with human accountability;
- communicate environmental and social commitments with measurable evidence rather than vague claims;
- invest in learning, respectful disagreement, partnership, and durable capability.

Every factual environmental, ethical, accessibility, social, or performance claim must have an entry in `docs/content/public-claims-register.md` before publication.
