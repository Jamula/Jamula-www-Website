## Control rules

- Jamula is the controller for its prospect/customer, portal, CRM and billing-purpose decisions. A customer may be controller for content it connects; Jamula may then be its processor under the contract/DPA. Record the role per tenant and purpose rather than applying one global label.
- `Contract`, `steps requested before contract`, `legitimate interests`, `legal obligation`, and `consent` below are **candidate bases**, not legal conclusions. Counsel confirms each served jurisdiction, whether an LIA/DPIA is required, and whether consent is freely given and withdrawable.
- No sale/share for cross-context behavioral advertising; no targeted advertising/profiling decisioning. Honor applicable universal opt-out signals, including GPC, by suppressing nonessential advertising/analytics uses. The launch baseline is no nonessential cookies until opt-in where consent is required.
- Jamula does not knowingly target minors. Do not collect date of birth. If a user discloses that they are below the locally approved minimum age, stop processing, preserve only necessary compliance evidence and follow the approved deletion/escalation procedure.
- Regions are desired deployment constraints, not current facts: prefer customer-selected US or EU geography; do not use a Global AI deployment for residency-sensitive tenants. Record actual storage and processing locations during procurement and tenant onboarding.
- The inventory, rights workflow, GPC handling, minimization, consent records, deletion and transfer map are Jamula's **applicable internal global control baseline**. They are not a conclusion that GDPR, UK GDPR, PECR, CCPA or another regime applies. EU/EEA GDPR and UK GDPR/PECR applicability, representative/DPO, ROPA/DPIA, breach and transfer duties remain `needs counsel` under `docs/legal/jurisdiction-matrix.md`; Cyrus must first approve served/targeted markets and monitoring/campaign facts.

## Retention authority

The retention cells below describe proposed **active-system or authoritative-record** schedules and remain subject to contract and professional approval. They do not define backup retention. [`backup-recovery.md`'s classification-specific schedule](../security/backup-recovery.md#authoritative-classification-specific-backup-schedule) is the single controlling backup/recovery-point schedule:

- C0 public/reproducible and C1 non-personal configuration may use the documented daily/weekly/monthly recovery points only when the point contains no personal/customer data or secrets.
- C2 personal/customer/tenant backups, C3 recovery points when exceptionally justified, and personal-data C4 backup copies have a **35-day maximum**; C3 has no routine backup by default.
- C4 authoritative financial, consent/suppression and security records follow a separately approved record schedule; a long-lived authoritative archive is not a product backup.
- C5 legal holds are precisely scoped, segregated from ordinary restore/product use, access-logged, reviewed and deleted/resumed under qualified-counsel direction.

Every restore is quarantined and non-serving until current deletion, suppression, consent-withdrawal, account-closure and connector-revocation tombstones replay and current authorization/ACL is verified. If ledger freshness or ordering is unproven, promotion fails closed. Customer wording must disclose that ordinary encrypted personal/customer backup copies expire within 35 days, deleted data cannot return to service after restore, and narrowly retained authoritative/legal-hold records may remain only on an approved basis.

## Processor and subprocessor acceptance record

Before any vendor processes production data, record:

1. legal entity, role by data set, services/features, DPA/product terms and contract version;
2. storage and processing regions, support/telemetry access, onward transfers, adequacy/SCC/UK Addendum/IDTA and TIA disposition;
3. complete current subprocessors, locations/functions, change-notice and objection/termination path;
4. encryption, tenant isolation, access, deletion/backup expiry, export, breach-notification SLA, audit reports and annual review;
5. model/data-use, human review, training/improvement, abuse-monitoring and optional stateful-feature settings for AI;
6. tested exit owner, format, duration, cost and deletion evidence.

The public notice must name or categorize recipients accurately and link a maintained subprocessor list. Vendor marketing or a generic security page is not sufficient contract evidence.
