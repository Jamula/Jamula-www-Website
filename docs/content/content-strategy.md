## Outcomes and guardrails

Jamula content should help prospective customers understand the problem Jamula can help solve, evaluate fit, and choose a low-friction next step. It should demonstrate useful technical judgment without exposing confidential work or turning aspirations into claims.

- Use `Jamula.net` as the canonical source. Adapt from it; do not create contradictory channel-specific facts.
- Describe Jamula as technology consulting, custom software, AI-native delivery, Microsoft-preferred, and multi-cloud capable only after Fact Checker confirms the exact public wording and Cyrus approves it.
- Do not imply Microsoft or any other vendor endorses Jamula.
- Do not publish internal workplace information, customer-confidential information, personal contact data, invented results, or unapproved founder facts.
- Environmental, ethical, social, accessibility, customer, AI, and performance claims are blocked until a complete entry in `public-claims-register.md` is verified by Fact Checker, reviewed by Sarek and Rai, and approved by Cyrus.
- A platform metric is an observation, not proof of customer value, accessibility, quality, safety, or business impact.
- No copy in this document is approved public copy.

Audience assumptions require validation through consent-respecting aggregate analytics and qualified conversations. They are not demographic targeting profiles.

## Editorial pillars

1. **Customer problems and service narratives** — the situation, stakes, bounded service, approach, deliverables, exclusions, evidence, and next step.
2. **Engineering and decision practice** — explain tradeoffs, portability, accessibility, privacy, security, and operational ownership without claiming outcomes not measured.
3. **Projects and case studies** — approved, attributable evidence with customer permission and defined baselines.
4. **Useful field notes** — current technical guidance tied to primary sources, versions, dates, and limitations.
5. **Company and founder** — only facts selected from controlled source registers and separately approved for exact wording.
6. **Responsible delivery** — describe mechanisms and planned tests; public values or outcome claims remain claims-register gated.

## Service-narrative template

Every service narrative must contain:

1. named audience and problem;
2. customer outcome framed as an objective, not a guaranteed result;
3. in-scope activities and concrete deliverables;
4. dependencies, customer responsibilities, exclusions, and escalation points;
5. privacy, security, accessibility, environmental/social, cost, and portability considerations;
6. evidence class and linked evidence;
7. prohibited or unsupported claims identified during review;
8. a single accessible call to action; and
9. owner, reviewers, approval record, publication date, review date, and expiry.

Pricing, availability, performance, security, sustainability, compatibility, or vendor statements must be checked against current official sources. Guarantees, superlatives, and comparative superiority language are prohibited unless their precise scope and substantiation are approved.

## Project and case-study workflow

| Gate | Required evidence | Blocking condition |
|---|---|---|
| Candidate intake | Business purpose, audience, project owner, proposed public value | No accountable owner |
| Customer permission | Written authorization for names, logos, quotations, screenshots, results, and channels | Missing, ambiguous, expired, or revoked permission |
| Confidentiality review | Contract/NDA check; removal of credentials, personal data, customer data, internal architecture, and security-sensitive details | Sarek or customer flags exposure |
| Claim design | Exact claim, baseline, time period, method, sample, limitations, owner, expiry, correction action | Claim-register entry incomplete |
| Evidence review | Fact Checker verifies source and reproduction; Rai reviews social/ethical framing | Evidence is anecdotal, vendor marketing, or unreproducible |
| Experience review | Dax reviews structure, accessibility, and visual communication | Critical accessibility barrier |
| Approval | Customer approval where required and Cyrus approval of exact version | Any approval missing |
| Publication/monitoring | Versioned canonical page, channel adaptations, analytics plan, expiry job | No takedown contact or expiry |

An anonymized story is not automatically safe: combinations of dates, sector, architecture, or results may re-identify a customer. Anonymization requires privacy review and cannot replace contractual permission.

## Measurement and learning

- Record a content ID, channel, objective, target audience, canonical URL, campaign parameters, owner, publication time, expiry, and approved metric before publication.
- Use aggregate, data-minimized analytics subject to the approved consent and privacy design. Do not upload contact lists or combine platform identities without an approved purpose, notice, and lawful basis.
- Review a monthly scorecard: items published, on-time review rate, expired-item rate, accessibility pass rate, rights-record completeness, correction/takedown SLO, moderation volume, qualified referrals, and contact task completion.
- Quarterly, retain, revise, retire, or test each content theme. Do not optimize for outrage, addictive engagement, volume, or vanity metrics.
- Baselines are established during the first approved 90-day implementation period. Targets are set only after data quality and consent behavior are validated.

## Evidence states

| State | Meaning | Permitted language |
|---|---|---|
| `verified narrow official claim` | A current official source was retrieved and supports the exact recorded statement | “The official source states…” with retrieval date, scope, version/region, and limits |
| `vendor-documented` | A vendor source describes its own terms, feature, metric, or behavior | Attribute to the vendor; not production, legal-applicability, or outcome evidence |
| `partial` | Only part of the source or claim was retrievable or supported | Use only the supported fragment; unresolved scope remains blocked |
| `access-blocked` | The exact source could not be retrieved because of access controls or transport behavior | Not verified; obtain an official alternative or preserve a blocked packet |
| `unverified` | No current source supports the exact statement | Do not publish the statement |
| `needs investigation` | Currentness, applicability, conflict, or replacement terms require more work | Do not rely on the unresolved point for publication |
| `contradicted` | Current evidence conflicts with the proposed statement | Remove/correct; formal reviewer rejection may apply |
| `mechanism evidence` | A bounded, disposable test exercised one mechanism | “In this test configuration…”; never a production or outcome claim |
| `future test` | A measurable acceptance test is specified but has not run | “Planned” internally only; no public result language |

Source status attaches to each claim, not to a page or source list as a whole. Reachability alone is not verification, and access-blocked or unretrieved material cannot be called verified.
